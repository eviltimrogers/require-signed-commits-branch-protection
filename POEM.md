# Ode to the Shetland Sheepdog

With silken coat and gentle eyes,
A Sheltie watches, swift and wise,
A streak of sable, gold, and white,
A herding heart, a pure delight.

Born on windswept islands far,
Where rolling hills and cold seas are,
They learned to tend the flock with care,
A loyal shadow everywhere.

So nimble-pawed and bright of mind,
No smarter breed you'll ever find,
They spin and dash and circle round,
The finest friend that can be found.

With feathered mane that frames their face,
They move through life with quiet grace,
A bark that rings across the moor,
A Sheltie's love is ever sure.

So here's to you, dear little friend,
Whose faithful heart will never bend,
The Shetland Sheepdog, brave and true,
The world is bright because of you.
