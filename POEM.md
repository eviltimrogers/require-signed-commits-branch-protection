# The Vigilant Sheltie

Upon the hill at break of day,
A sable shadow stands in gray,
With amber eyes that pierce the mist,
No movement, sound, or sight is missed.

The Sheltie watches, ever keen,
A guardian of the pastures green,
Her mane of gold and chestnut flame
Declares from whence her lineage came.

She circles wide, then circles near,
To keep her flock from doubt and fear,
No lamb may stray, no ewe run loose,
Beneath her watch, there is no excuse.

The fox may creep through morning dew,
The crow may call — she sees him too,
Alert and poised on nimble feet,
Her vigilance cannot be beat.

When thunder rolls across the sky
And restless sheep begin to cry,
She barks her calm, commanding call,
And gathers one, and gathers all.

At dusk she rests, but still one eye
Is open to the starlit sky,
For even in her dreaming deep,
The Sheltie tends her beloved sheep.
